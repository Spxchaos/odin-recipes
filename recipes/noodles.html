Hakka Noodles aka Desi Chow Mein

Home

The pinnacle of Indo-Chinese cuisine. One of the most beloved indian street foods. Spicy Saucy Delicious noodles.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet noodles
  • 1 carrot, finely sliced
  • 1 onion, finely sliced
  • 1/2 cabbage, finely chopped
  • 1 capsicum, finely sliced
  • 4-5 french beans, finely chopped
  • 1 masala mix with MSG

Method

  1. Firstly boil 4 cups of water.
  2. Add 1 tsp salt and oil to it.
  3. Once it begins to boil, lower the flame and dip inside the noodles and cook them al dente. This will take only 5-7 minutes.
  4. Transfer them on a sieve to drain off the excess water and wash with cold water.
  5. In a wok, add some oil and heat it.
  6. Saute onion.
  7. Now add the rest of the vegetables and saute for about a minute.
  8. Add the masala mix with MSG.
  9. Mix them well.
  10. Garnish with some chopped green onions and serve hot.

And Viola!, your hot and spicy desi style chinese noodles are ready.
